Our People

Meet the Team

A seasoned crew with decades of combined experience in video, design, sound, and storytelling.

Andy McGrath

Director / Producer / Chef

Andy is renowned for making videos easy, fun and memorable. An experienced TV industry Director/Cameraman, Series Editor, music composer and former owner of Auckland-based film and motion graphics company Afterglow Films, Andy brings together skills from over 25 years of crafting professional video and television.

Duncan McCann

Creative Director / Sauce Reduction

The secret sauce for any good communication is a good creative concept that reduces information clutter to the essence of what's required. After 20 years as a writer, producer, and strategist in Advertising and Communications Agencies, Duncan lends creative and analytical firepower to crafting messages and understanding audiences.

Gary Leano

Graphic FX Director / Hot Sauce

Gary has spent decades at the top of his game providing specialist motion graphics, animation, and compositing for advertising, branding and promos for broadcasters like Sky Sport, TVNZ, TV3, Whakaata Maori, and a huge range of Vision8 client projects.

Kat Greagar

Photography / Underwater Camera / Stylish Sauce

Kat has over 20 years of experience in commercial and creative photography, creative design, sales management, and branding. Her lifelong connection with the moana provides Vision8 with an unsurpassed underwater camera experience.

Jeramiah Ross

Audio Design / Composition / Saucy Sounds

A genius in the sound studio, Jeramiah has provided sound design and music composition for award-winning creative companies, including Weta Workshop, Dreamworks, RESN, PIKPOK, Activision, and Magic Leap. He composed the soundtrack for the 'Send Yourself to Wellington' campaign and scored the video game Shatter.
